# 1. Objective

Successfully launch the website to production and establish an operational framework for continuous improvement, content publishing, and future feature development.

---

# 2. Production Launch Checklist

## Technical
- Production deployment successful
- HTTPS enabled
- Custom domain configured
- DNS verified
- Sitemap submitted
- robots.txt verified

## Quality
- Lighthouse >90
- No broken links
- Contact form tested
- Mobile responsiveness verified
- Accessibility audit completed

## Content
- Homepage complete
- About page complete
- FinAgent AI project page live
- Resume available
- Minimum three blog posts published
